Design Vault mod for the Steam game - make targets (Windows; details in design-vault/README.md)

  make mod                          build mod/out/designvault.wz from your stored designs
  make mod-install                  build it and install it into your Warzone 2100 profile
  make mod-uninstall                remove it again (your library and lines are kept)
  make play-mod                     install, start the Steam game with the mod, rebuild when you quit
  make remove-design NAME="..."     remove a saved design for good (library, stored file, lines)
  make test-mod                     the unmodified Steam exe with the mod in a scripted skirmish
